\pgheader[DoriaBlue]{Ottaviano Doria}{Head of House / The Admiral}{Doria}
\pgquote{The sea asks no permission. Neither do I.}

\section*{Background \& Drive}

\begin{rulebox}{}
  \textbf{Background:} Fifty-two years old, with scars from naval battles fought in
  Greek waters before he was twenty. He is the family's public face in Genoa ---
  the man the French invite to banquets, who smiles while planning their downfall.

  \medskip\noindent\textcolor{DoriaBlue}{\textbf{Drive:}} To see Genoa free before
  he dies. Not ideology --- pride. The Doria have ruled the sea for three hundred
  years. He will not tolerate a foreign king telling them where to dock.
\end{rulebox}
