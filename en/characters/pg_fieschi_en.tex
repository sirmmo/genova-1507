% PC 1: NICCOLÒ FIESCHI
\pgheader[FieschiGreen]{Niccol\`o Fieschi}{The Count / The Feudal Lord}{Fieschi}
\pgquote{I inherited three centuries of nobility. I am obliged to use them.}

\section*{Background \& Drive}
\begin{rulebox}{}
\textbf{Background:} Forty-five years old, Count of Lavagna, lord of estates
stretching as far as the Ronco pass. He is not a city man --- he is a man of
hills, castles, and jurisdictions that trace back to Charlemagne. In Genoa he
moves with the awkwardness of someone who knows he has authority but isn't quite
sure how to exercise it between narrow streets.

\medskip\noindent\textcolor{FieschiGreen}{\textbf{Drive:}} Dignity. He cannot bear
the humiliation of watching his vassals taxed by foreigners. His name is older
than Louis XII's.
\end{rulebox}

\section*{Special Ability}
\begin{fieschibox}{Feudal Authority}
When Niccol\`o invokes his authority in the name of the Fieschi house and feudal
law, NPCs of lower rank (vassals, craftsmen, commoners) cannot refuse without
immediate consequences. Also works on non-French Genoese soldiers.
For NPCs of equal or higher rank, requires a Command roll.
\end{fieschibox}

% PC 2: MONSIGNOR GREGORIO FIESCHI
\pgheader[FieschiGreen]{Monsignor Gregorio Fieschi}{The Auxiliary Bishop / The Church's Voice}{Fieschi}
\pgquote{Prayer moves mountains. Politics moves them where they're needed.}

\section*{Background \& Drive}
\begin{rulebox}{}
\textbf{Background:} Forty-two years old, Niccol\`o's cousin, Auxiliary Bishop of
Genoa. He controls San Lorenzo and four minor churches. Not a mystic, not a reformer
--- an ecclesiastical administrator with precise ambitions. He wants to become
Archbishop.

\medskip\noindent\textcolor{FieschiGreen}{\textbf{Drive:}} The Archbishopric. The
revolt is the chance to demonstrate that the Fieschi are Genoa's defenders of the
faith --- and to earn recognition from the Holy See.
\end{rulebox}

\section*{Special Ability}
\begin{fieschibox}{Interdict and Absolution}
Gregorio may declare a place or person under ecclesiastical Interdict. Catholic
NPCs (virtually everyone in Genoa) refuse to interact with the target until the
Interdict is lifted. He may also publicly Absolve someone, immediately restoring
their social acceptability. \textit{Once per direction per session.}
\end{fieschibox}

% PC 3: FRA' TOMASO
\pgheader[FieschiGreen]{Fra' Tomaso of Bologna}{The Preacher / The People's Voice}{Fieschi}
\pgquote{I don't speak for the Fieschi. I speak for those without a voice. Sometimes they coincide.}

\section*{Background \& Drive}
\begin{rulebox}{}
\textbf{Background:} Thirty-four years old, Dominican, arrived in Genoa from Tuscany
two years ago. He is not a Fieschi --- he is an ally by circumstance. His sermons
against the French occupation have become legendary in the popular quarters. The
Fieschi use him, he uses the Fieschi; both know the alliance has an expiry date.

\medskip\noindent\textcolor{FieschiGreen}{\textbf{Drive:}} Justice --- real, not
aristocratic. He wants a free Genoa to also be a just Genoa, where harbour workers
and craftsmen have a voice in governance. This puts him on a collision course with
nearly every noble who is currently using him.
\end{rulebox}

% PC 4: MARGHERITA THE WIDOW
\pgheader[FieschiGreen]{Margherita the Widow}{Quarter Leader / The Streets' Voice}{Fieschi}
\pgquote{You nobles have your palaces. We have the streets. And streets don't lie.}

\section*{Background \& Drive}
\begin{rulebox}{}
\textbf{Background:} Forty-eight years old, widow of a cobbler who died of fever ten
years ago. No titles, no money, no formal education. She has something rarer: the
respect of three \textit{sestieri} of Genoa, built over twenty years of mediation,
protecting the vulnerable, and solving the practical problems that nobles consider
too small to bother with. The Fieschi sought her out --- not the other way around.

\medskip\noindent\textcolor{FieschiGreen}{\textbf{Drive:}} That her children and her
neighbours' children do not grow up in an occupied city. She is not a revolutionary
--- she is an exasperated mother with access to three hundred people willing to listen.
\end{rulebox}

civilians, requires a Consort roll.
