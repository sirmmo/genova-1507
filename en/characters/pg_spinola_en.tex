% ────── PC 1: RAFFAELE SPINOLA ──────────────────────────────
\pgheader[SpinolaRed]{Raffaele Spinola the Elder}{The Banker / The Patriarch}{Spinola}
\pgquote{Money has no smell. Power does --- and I know every shade of it.}

\section*{Background \& Drive}
\begin{rulebox}{}
\textbf{Background:} Fifty-eight years old, the family's true head. Rarely visible
in public --- he operates through intermediaries, letters, and a network of debtors
spanning half of Europe. He has financed three wars, two conclaves, and a Roman
palace that bears someone else's name. He prefers it that way.

\medskip\noindent\textcolor{SpinolaRed}{\textbf{Drive:}} Stability. He doesn't hate
the French on principle --- he hates them because they destabilise markets and make
debts unpredictable. A free Genoa is a Genoa where contracts are honoured.
\end{rulebox}

  \azione{Study}{Studio}{4}{Accounting, commercial law, codes}

  \azione{Tinker}{Ingegno}{2}{Systems, organisational structures}
\end{attributoblock}

\begin{spinolabox}{The Debt Ledger}
Raffaele may declare that any NPC owes a debt to the Spinola family. The NPC is
obligated to honour a reasonable request. An unreasonable request requires a Sway
roll from Controlled Position. \textit{Three times per session. NPCs only.}
\end{spinolabox}

\begin{goldlist}

\item Spinola wax seal --- a document stamped with this is worth gold

\medskip\noindent\textcolor{SecretRed}{\textbf{Agenda:}} \textit{Control the outcome

% ────── PC 2: LUCREZIA ──────────────────────────────────────

\begin{rulebox}{}
\textbf{Background:} Thirty-two years old, born a Doria --- a marriage of convenience
she has turned into a weapon. Intermediary between the Spinola and foreign courts,

health.'' Everyone knows it is a lie. No one knows the truth.

\medskip\noindent\textcolor{SpinolaRed}{\textbf{Drive:}} Loyalty frightens her: she
has too much of it towards too many people. She wants the revolt to resolve the
conflict so she can finally choose which side to stand on.

% ────── PC 3: SER BENEDETTO ─────────────────────────────────
\pgheader[SpinolaRed]{Ser Benedetto Adorno}{The Notary / Keeper of Secrets}{Spinola}
\pgquote{I have written contracts that changed the fate of cities. No one knows my name.}

\section*{Background \& Drive}
\begin{rulebox}{}
\textbf{Background:} Fifty-two years old, notary at the Palazzo di San Giorgio, the
Spinola's trusted man for twenty years. He keeps the bank's real ledgers --- not the
ones shown to the authorities. He is a deeply honest man in a role that demands
absolute dishonesty towards the outside world. This contradiction is consuming him.

\medskip\noindent\textcolor{SpinolaRed}{\textbf{Drive:}} He wants the revolt to end
in a way that lets him stop lying. A free Genoa with new institutions might let him
make the true accounts public without risking his life.
\end{rulebox}

do not. \textit{Can also be used to fabricate evidence against someone.}

% ────── PC 4: MARGHERITA THE CAT ────────────────────────────
\pgheader[SpinolaRed]{Margherita ``la Gatta''}{Field Agent / The Informant}{Spinola}
\pgquote{I'm not a spy. I'm a woman who pays attention. Others make the distinction.}

\section*{Background \& Drive}
\begin{rulebox}{}
\textbf{Background:} Thirty-nine years old, raised in the backstreets of Soziglia.
She entered the Spinola network by accident --- she overheard something she should
not have, and instead of being eliminated she was recruited. Since then she is their
most capable field agent: she knows the \textit{caruggi}, the taverns, the markets,
and the secrets circulating in each of them.

\medskip\noindent\textcolor{SpinolaRed}{\textbf{Drive:}} A place of her own, enough
money to make choices, and no one controlling her. The revolt is the chance to move
from survival to freedom.
\end{rulebox}
