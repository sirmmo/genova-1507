\pgheader[DoriaBlue]{Elena Doria}{The Galley Commander / The Armed Wing}{Doria}
\pgquote{My father wanted a male heir. He got me instead. He adapted.}

\section*{Background \& Drive}
\begin{rulebox}{}
\textbf{Background:} Thirty-four years old, daughter of a galley captain killed in battle,
raised on ships before palaces. She commands formally as ``cargo superintendent'' ---
the diplomatic cover that lets a woman stand on a command deck. Her sailors call her
simply \textit{la Comandante}. It is not an official title. It is worth more than one.

\medskip\noindent\textcolor{DoriaBlue}{\textbf{Drive:}} The galley she commands is the
only thing she inherited from her father. The French have taxed its profits, reduced its
routes, and humiliated her crew at customs checkpoints. She has waited long enough.
\end{rulebox}

constitution guaranteeing the rights of the great families. Not the revolt itself ---
the order that comes after. He is the only person at this table thinking systematically
