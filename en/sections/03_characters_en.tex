\charheader{doria}{Ottaviano Doria}{Head of House / The Admiral}%
  {The sea asks no permission. Neither do I.}

\begin{multicols}{2}
\subsection*{Background \& Drive}
Fifty-two years old, with scars from naval battles fought before he was
twenty. He is the family's public face in Genoa --- the man the French
invite to banquets, who smiles while planning their downfall.

\textbf{\textcolor{DoriaBlue}{Drive:}} \textit{To see Genoa free before
he dies. Not ideology --- pride. The Doria have ruled the sea for
three hundred years. He will not tolerate a foreign king telling them
where to dock.}
\columnbreak
\subsection*{Stress \& Trauma}
\clocktrack[DoriaBlue]{STRESS}{0}{9}
\textit{\small Trauma: □ Cold \ □ Haunted \ □ Obsessed \ □ Paranoid \ □ Shattered}
\end{multicols}

%   [ fitd:actionName "Tinker" ; fitd:rating 0 ] .

\statrow{Study} {1}{4}{Tactical analysis, naval history, reading enemy intent}
\statrow{Survey}{2}{4}{Assess situations, scan the political horizon}
\statrow{Tinker}{0}{4}{Mechanisms, artillery, naval engineering}

\attrblock{doria}{PROWESS --- Body}

\bondentry{Bianca}{My niece. Sharper than anyone in this room. I'd die for her, but she doesn't need me to.}
\bondentry{Fra' Matteo}{I pulled him out of a Turkish galley in '78. He owes me something he can never repay --- and it weighs on me.}
\bondentry{Giacomo the Moor}{I don't trust him. But he's the best at what he does.}

\subsection*{Equipment}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Spanish-made side-sword --- a gift from someone he no longer remembers
  \item Secret nautical charts of Genoa harbour with the real depths (not the ones shown to the French)
  \item The family seal --- worth more than a hundred swords
  \item Silver flask of local grappa. He never offers anyone a drink.
\end{itemize}

\begin{secretbox}
% @rpg-schema: pg:OttavianoDoria rpg:hasSecret
%   "Secretly mortgaged two galleys with a pro-French banker three years ago. If the House Heist fails, the banker can seize them." .
Three years ago, during a liquidity crisis, Ottaviano secretly mortgaged
two of the family's four galleys to a Genoese banker with French sympathies.
If the House Heist fails and the debt isn't covered, that banker can seize
the ships --- and he will. No other Doria knows.

\medskip
\textbf{\textcolor{SecretRed}{Hidden Agenda:}} Complete the House Heist at any personal cost.
Sacrifice reputation, sacrifice a companion if necessary --- but never the ships.
\textit{Never the ships.}
\end{secretbox}

\charheader{doria}{Ser Fulvio Doria}{The Lieutenant / The Fixer}%
  {A man who never lies doesn't survive in this city. I survive.}

\begin{multicols}{2}
\subsection*{Background \& Drive}
Forty-five years old, second cousin to the Admiral. He has run the family's
dirty operations for twenty years --- the customs bribes, the inconvenient
disappearances, the negotiations that must never be recorded.

\textbf{\textcolor{DoriaBlue}{Drive:}} \textit{Loyalty to the Doria, with
a private reservation: loyalty has a price, and certain people still owe
him. The revolt is a chance to collect.}
\columnbreak
\subsection*{Stress \& Trauma}
\clocktrack[DoriaBlue]{STRESS}{0}{9}
\textit{\small Trauma: □ Cold \ □ Haunted \ □ Obsessed \ □ Paranoid \ □ Shattered}
\end{multicols}

\attrblock{doria}{INSIGHT}
\statrow{Hunt}  {2}{4}{Find people --- anyone, anywhere}
\statrow{Study} {1}{4}{Read documents; evaluate testimony}
\statrow{Survey}{3}{4}{Nothing escapes his notice --- especially lies}
\statrow{Tinker}{1}{4}{Occasional lockpick; simple mechanisms}

\attrblock{doria}{PROWESS}
\statrow{Finesse}{2}{4}{Pickpocket, poison, fast hands}
\statrow{Prowl}  {3}{4}{Surveillance, tailing, vanishing in a crowd}
\statrow{Skirmish}{2}{4}{Knife, close-quarters dirty fighting}
\statrow{Wreck}  {0}{4}{Not his approach}

\attrblock{doria}{RESOLVE}
\statrow{Attune} {1}{4}{Senses the tension in a room}
\statrow{Command}{1}{4}{Issues orders to subordinates}
\statrow{Consort}{2}{4}{Taverns, brothels, and places where people talk}
\statrow{Sway}   {3}{4}{Convinces, threatens, blackmails with elegance}

\abilitybox{doria}{The Shadow's Informant}{%
  Once per session, Fulvio may declare that he already arranged a useful
  resource in the current scene --- a bribed witness, an escape route, a
  compromising document --- without having announced it beforehand.%
}{No roll required. The resource exists. The GM determines how it enters play.}

\subsection*{Bonds}
\bondentry{Ottaviano}{The Admiral believes I'm loyal beyond question. He's almost right. There's one thing he doesn't know --- and can't.}
\bondentry{Bianca}{The niece frightens me. She sees too much. She looked at me once in a way that told me she knows something.}
\bondentry{Fra' Matteo}{I use him for my most delicate messages. He knows that if he talked, he'd never see his priory again.}
\bondentry{Giacomo}{We grew up together in the \textit{caruggi}. He's my real cousin, blood-kin. I'd protect him against any order.}

\subsection*{Equipment}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Stiletto hidden in his right boot
  \item Three false-identity documents, ready to use
  \item A coded list of the family's debtors and creditors --- only he can read it
  \item Key to a house in the Soziglia \textit{sestiere} that officially doesn't exist
\end{itemize}

\begin{secretbox}
% @rpg-schema: pg:FulvioDoria rpg:hasSecret
%   "In contact with the Spinola: believes he is using them, but the last intelligence he passed was true and damaged the Doria." .
Fulvio is in contact with the Spinola. He's convinced himself he's using
them --- feeding them only what he wants them to know. But the last
piece of intelligence he passed was genuine, and it damaged the Doria.

\medskip
\textbf{\textcolor{SecretRed}{Hidden Agenda:}} Secure a personal exit regardless of the revolt's
outcome. He has already negotiated his own survival with a third party.
If the Doria win, fine. If they fall, Fulvio doesn't fall with them.
\end{secretbox}

\charheader{doria}{Bianca Doria}{The Strategist / The Shadow Heir}%
  {My grandfather commands the galleys. I command my grandfather.}

\begin{multicols}{2}
\subsection*{Background \& Drive}
Twenty-seven years old, Ottaviano's niece, daughter of his elder brother
lost at sea a decade ago. She should have counted for nothing --- a woman,
in a man's world. Instead she counts for everything. She planned the House
Heist. She identified the French convoy's route. Ottaviano knows, and
calls her ``my little niece'' in front of others.

\textbf{\textcolor{DoriaBlue}{Drive:}} \textit{To prove --- to herself
first --- that the Doria can thrive when the right people lead,
not merely the ones with the longest sword.}
\columnbreak
\subsection*{Stress \& Trauma}
\clocktrack[DoriaBlue]{STRESS}{0}{9}
\textit{\small Trauma: □ Cold \ □ Haunted \ □ Obsessed \ □ Paranoid \ □ Shattered}
\end{multicols}

\attrblock{doria}{INSIGHT}
\statrow{Hunt}  {1}{4}{Finds patterns; researches methodically}
\statrow{Study} {4}{4}{Deep analysis, languages (Latin, French, Arabic), cartography}
\statrow{Survey}{3}{4}{Reads rooms, people, and political situations}
\statrow{Tinker}{2}{4}{Codes, mechanisms, logistical traps}

\attrblock{doria}{PROWESS}
\statrow{Finesse}{2}{4}{Writing, drawing, poisons when necessary}
\statrow{Prowl}  {1}{4}{Moves through social environments without being noticed}
\statrow{Skirmish}{0}{4}{Avoids direct confrontation --- not the right solution}
\statrow{Wreck}  {0}{4}{No}

\attrblock{doria}{RESOLVE}
\statrow{Attune} {2}{4}{Reads emotions; senses hidden dynamics}
\statrow{Command}{2}{4}{Leads through logic, not volume}
\statrow{Consort}{3}{4}{At home in salons, negotiations, alliance-building}
\statrow{Sway}   {3}{4}{Persuades rationally or emotionally, as needed}

\abilitybox{doria}{Two Moves Ahead}{%
  When Bianca \textit{plans} an action (rather than executing it herself),
  she may declare a \textbf{Contingency}: if the action fails,
  the contingency triggers automatically, no roll required.%
}{Must be declared before the other PC rolls. Narratively plausible contingencies only. Once per Heist.}

\subsection*{Bonds}
\bondentry{Ottaviano}{I love him like a father and respect him like a general. But he's ageing. Some decisions are mine now. He knows. He doesn't say it.}
\bondentry{Ser Fulvio}{I care for him like an uncle. Which is exactly why he frightens me --- I know he's lying, just not yet about what.}
\bondentry{Fra' Matteo}{The only one who doesn't underestimate me. Perhaps because he's old enough not to care about being wrong.}
\bondentry{Giacomo}{Useful. Dangerous. I don't trust him, but he has access to channels we don't.}

\subsection*{Equipment}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Coded notebook containing the full House Heist plan
  \item Letter signed by a Venetian senator --- an outstanding favour to call in
  \item Men's clothing for moving through districts closed to women
  \item Small vial of non-lethal sleeping draught --- ``for emergencies''
\end{itemize}

\begin{secretbox}
% @rpg-schema: pg:BiancaDoria rpg:hasSecret
%   "Has established contact with the Grimaldi for a family escape route to Monaco if the revolt fails. The price is not yet accepted." .
Bianca has already opened a line to the Grimaldi --- not to betray the
Doria, but to secure an escape route if the revolt collapses. The deal:
the Doria could take refuge in Monaco. She hasn't yet agreed to the Grimaldi's
price, but they are waiting for her answer.

\medskip
\textbf{\textcolor{SecretRed}{Hidden Agenda:}} Guarantee the House's long-term survival above
all else. If the House Heist endangers more people than it saves,
Bianca will abandon the plan and propose an alternative --- even against
Ottaviano's explicit wishes.
\end{secretbox}

\charheader{doria}{Fra' Matteo da Recco}{The Chaplain / The Conscience}%
  {God forgives. I keep the tally.}

\begin{multicols}{2}
\subsection*{Background \& Drive}
Sixty-one years old, Dominican, fleet chaplain to the Doria for three
decades. He has seen enough naval battles, shipboard epidemics, and
deathbed confessions to have no illusions left about human nature.
He still believes in God. He is no longer sure he believes in people.

\textbf{\textcolor{DoriaBlue}{Drive:}} \textit{A Christian people
cannot be governed by foreign powers without the Church's consent.
The Pope never blessed the French occupation. Therefore the revolt is
just. The logic is airtight.}
\columnbreak
\subsection*{Stress \& Trauma}
\clocktrack[DoriaBlue]{STRESS}{0}{9}
\textit{\small Trauma: □ Cold \ □ Haunted \ □ Obsessed \ □ Paranoid \ □ Shattered}
\end{multicols}

\attrblock{doria}{INSIGHT}
\statrow{Hunt}  {0}{4}{Not his field}
\statrow{Study} {3}{4}{Theology, medicine, ecclesiastical history, Latin and Greek}
\statrow{Survey}{2}{4}{Reads people as if studying their souls}
\statrow{Tinker}{1}{4}{Prepares medicaments; small chemistry}

\attrblock{doria}{PROWESS}
\statrow{Finesse}{1}{4}{Steady surgeon's hands}
\statrow{Prowl}  {0}{4}{Not his speciality}
\statrow{Skirmish}{0}{4}{He does not fight. Not any more.}
\statrow{Wreck}  {0}{4}{No.}

\attrblock{doria}{RESOLVE}
\statrow{Attune} {3}{4}{Feels the spiritual and emotional currents of a group}
\statrow{Command}{2}{4}{Moral authority can outweigh military authority}
\statrow{Consort}{3}{4}{Confraternities, religious orders, lower clergy}
\statrow{Sway}   {2}{4}{The preacher's voice. Used rarely, devastatingly effective.}

\abilitybox{doria}{Confession \& Absolution}{%
  When a PC at the table is about to suffer Trauma, Fra' Matteo may intervene.
  The Trauma becomes a single point of Stress instead --- but the PC must
  confess to Fra' Matteo (briefly, in the scene) what brought them to that point.%
}{Once per PC per session. Fra' Matteo must be physically present in the scene.}

\subsection*{Bonds}
\bondentry{Ottaviano}{I've known him since his hair was black. He has carried burdens he should never have had to. I try to lighten them, knowing I never can.}
\bondentry{Ser Fulvio}{I don't trust him. Not because he's wicked --- because he's pragmatic in ways that challenge natural law. He confesses minor sins with such regularity that I wonder what he's hiding behind them.}
\bondentry{Bianca}{She is the future of this family. I pray for her every morning --- not because she needs it, but because the weight she carries is too heavy for someone so young.}
\bondentry{Giacomo}{A cheerful sinner. I prefer them to joyless saints. I love him like a prodigal son who never stops being lost.}

\subsection*{Equipment}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Breviary with loose pages inside --- coded messages
  \item Field medical kit: bandages, alcohol, needle and heavy thread
  \item A letter from the Bishop of Genoa authorising him to move freely through all \textit{sestieri}
  \item Olive-wood rosary --- the only thing remaining from his original priory
\end{itemize}

\begin{secretbox}
% @rpg-schema: pg:FraMatteo rpg:hasSecret
%   "Knows of Ottaviano's secret debt on the galleys through the banker's confession. The seal of the confessional paralyses him." .
Fra' Matteo knows about Ottaviano's secret debt on the galleys --- not from
Ottaviano's own confession, but from the banker's, who came to him for reasons
Fra' Matteo still cannot explain. The seal of the confessional immobilises him.
He cannot speak. But he might act indirectly to resolve the problem.

\medskip
\textbf{\textcolor{SecretRed}{Hidden Agenda:}} Protect Ottaviano from himself.
If the old admiral makes a decision that will destroy him --- sacrifice himself,
surrender to the French, something heroic and useless --- Fra' Matteo will
do everything in his power to prevent it, including disobeying direct orders.
\end{secretbox}

\charheader{doria}{Giacomo the Moor}{The Smuggler / The Street Man}%
  {I'm not one of yours. But tonight, perhaps I am.}

\begin{multicols}{2}
\subsection*{Background \& Drive}
Thirty-six years old, obscure origins (Genoese mother, Saracen father,
or so the story goes --- he doesn't deny it because it's useful).
Smuggler, broker, and occasional paid informant for whoever is buying.
The Doria use him for unofficial supplies and intelligence on French
harbour movements. He is not a member of the family. He is a tool with
his own agenda.

\textbf{\textcolor{DoriaBlue}{Drive:}} \textit{Money, first. But also ---
admitted only when drunk --- a dim sense that a free Genoa would be better
for people like him: those without a house, a name, or guarantees.}
\columnbreak
\subsection*{Stress \& Trauma}
\clocktrack[DoriaBlue]{STRESS}{0}{9}
\textit{\small Trauma: □ Cold \ □ Haunted \ □ Obsessed \ □ Paranoid \ □ Shattered}
\end{multicols}

\attrblock{doria}{INSIGHT}
\statrow{Hunt}  {3}{4}{Finds people, goods, information --- in any harbour}
\statrow{Study} {1}{4}{Languages: Arabic, Catalan, rough Greek}
\statrow{Survey}{3}{4}{Thirty years of survival depended on this}
\statrow{Tinker}{2}{4}{Boats, nets, knots, simple mechanisms}

\attrblock{doria}{PROWESS}
\statrow{Finesse}{2}{4}{Pickpocket, climbing, precise movement in tight spaces}
\statrow{Prowl}  {3}{4}{\textit{Caruggi}, rooftops, canals --- every unofficial route}
\statrow{Skirmish}{2}{4}{Knife, club, dirty fighting}
\statrow{Wreck}  {1}{4}{Breaks things when needed}

\attrblock{doria}{RESOLVE}
\statrow{Attune} {1}{4}{Animal instinct for danger}
\statrow{Command}{0}{4}{Does not command. Not in his vocabulary.}
\statrow{Consort}{2}{4}{Taverns, black markets, low company}
\statrow{Sway}   {2}{4}{Haggles, bluffs, fast-talks}

\abilitybox{doria}{These Alleys Are Mine}{%
  In the Prè \textit{sestiere}, the old harbour, or the \textit{caruggi} of
  the centre, Giacomo cannot be surprised. He always has an escape route.
  If pursued, he can make himself and one other person disappear with no roll.%
}{Always active in the listed districts. Outside them, requires a Prowl roll.}

\subsection*{Bonds}
% @rpg-schema: pg:GiacomoMoro fitd:hasBond
%   [ fitd:bondTarget pg:FulvioDoria ;
%     fitd:bondText "We're real cousins, blood kin. He forgets when convenient. I remember when convenient." ] .
\bondentry{Ottaviano}{He respects me the way he respects a useful tool. That's fine. Useful tools survive. Gentlemen, less so.}
\bondentry{Ser Fulvio}{We're real cousins. He forgets when it suits him. I remember when it suits me.}
\bondentry{Bianca}{The only Doria who talks to me like a person. I can't decide if I find that comforting or unsettling.}
\bondentry{Fra' Matteo}{I confess to him because he's the only one who's never surprised. And because he doesn't talk.}

\subsection*{Equipment}
\begin{itemize}
  \item A rowing boat hidden under Sampierdarena wharf --- only he knows where
  \item A mental map of all underground passages beneath the harbour (written nowhere)
  \item Hunting knife with a whale-bone handle
  \item Small pouch with coins in five different currencies --- always ready to run
\end{itemize}

\begin{secretbox}
% @rpg-schema: pg:GiacomoMoro rpg:hasSecret
%   "Has sold intelligence about the Doria to the Spinola --- including the French convoy route central to the House Heist." .
Giacomo has already sold Doria intelligence to the Spinola --- not the most
dangerous kind, just enough to keep a door open. But one piece of information
he passed was the French convoy's route --- the one at the heart of the
House Heist. If the Spinola use it, the Heist is compromised.

\medskip
\textbf{\textcolor{SecretRed}{Hidden Agenda:}} Survive and leave Genoa with enough
coin to start over somewhere else. The only thing that could stop him is Fulvio
--- if his cousin is in real trouble, Giacomo stays.
\end{secretbox}
