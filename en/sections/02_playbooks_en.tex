\familyheader{doria}{House Doria}%
  {The sea is ours. Genoa is ours.}%
  {Admirals, corsairs, shipbuilders --- masters of the Mediterranean.}

\begin{multicols}{2}

\section{Historical Identity}
\label{sec:doria-identity}

The \idx{Doria} are Genoa's pre-eminent naval family. Admirals, privateers,
and shipbuilders --- their name echoes across every port in the Mediterranean.
Traditionally \idx{Ghibelline}, they have fought the French when it suited them.
It suits them again.

Their strength is the \textbf{Fleet}: the galleys in the harbour answer to
the Doria before they answer to the French governor. Their blind spot is
arrogance --- they believe naval power solves every problem, and they
chronically underestimate the political manoeuvrings at which the Spinola excel.

\section{House Heist: The Fleet of Betrayal}
\label{sec:doria-heist}

% @rpg-schema: genova1507:FlottaDelTradimento a fitd:Mission ;
%   rdfs:label "The Fleet of Betrayal" ;
%   fitd:exclusiveTo genova1507:CasaDoria ;
%   fitd:clockAdvance "+2" ;
%   genova1507:vulnerability genova1507:CasaGrimaldi .

\begin{rulebox}[DoriaBlue]{Scenario}
A French convoy carrying the garrison's payroll is approaching the harbour.
The Doria can intercept it with their galleys, strip the French soldiers of
their wages (and their loyalty), and deliver the treasure to the revolt.
\end{rulebox}

\heistphase{1}{Reconnaissance}{Locate the convoy's route.
  \textbf{Roll: Awareness.} Failure: the French change course.}

\heistphase{2}{Interception}{The galleys intercept the convoy in Genoese waters.
  \textbf{Roll: Force.} Failure: naval engagement, Soldier losses.}

\heistphase{3}{Distribution}{The seized funds go to all Genoese houses
  (+2 Coin each), \textit{or} the Doria keep it all (+4 Coin, but --2
  Reputation with every other house).}

\textbf{Reward:} +2 to the Shared Clock. If distributed, +1 Reputation
with all houses.

\textit{Vulnerability: The Grimaldi know every merchant route. If they
interfere, Phase~1 automatically fails.}

interior, who have supplied bishops, cardinals, and popes to the Church for
centuries. Historically \idx{Guelph}. Their power is twofold: estates in

A single sermon from the right pulpit is worth more than a hundred armed men.

\section{Historical Identity}

The \idx{Grimaldi} are the only Genoese family with autonomous territory:
\idx{Monaco}, seized in 1297 when François Grimaldi entered the fortress
disguised as a Franciscan friar. They live in two worlds at once ---
Genoese by blood, sovereign lords in fact.

This makes them the most ambiguous players in the game. They have contacts
at the French court (Monaco borders French Provence), commercial ties across
the Mediterranean, and the genuine option of surviving \textit{regardless}
of the revolt's outcome. They can win even if Genoa loses.

\section{House Heist: The Price of Autonomy}
\label{sec:grimaldi-heist}

% @rpg-schema: genova1507:PrezzoAutonomia a fitd:Mission ;

\begin{rulebox}[GrimaldiPurple]{Scenario}
The Grimaldi make secret contact with Louis~XII's representative in Genoa.
Not to betray the revolt --- but to negotiate: in exchange for formal French
recognition of Monaco's sovereignty (long denied), the Grimaldi undertake
not to obstruct an \textit{orderly} French withdrawal.
Is this betrayal? Is it diplomacy? That depends entirely on who wins.
\end{rulebox}

\heistphase{1}{The secret channel}{Establish secure contact with the French
  representative without the other houses finding out.
  \textbf{Roll: Intrigue.} Failure: one house discovers the negotiation.}

\heistphase{2}{The proposal}{Present the terms.
  \textbf{Roll: Commerce} (treated as a commercial deal).
  Failure: the French demand too much, or simply don't trust the Grimaldi.}

\heistphase{3}{The agreement}{Monaco is formally recognised. The French
  withdraw ``in good order'' (the clock doesn't fall back for their
  withdrawal). If the other houses discover the deal: --3 Reputation.}

\textbf{Reward:} +2 to the clock. The Grimaldi secure Monaco's recognition ---
an independent victory condition met regardless of the clock's final state.

\textit{Vulnerability: The Spinola have an agent near the French representative.
They can intercept the Phase~1 message.}
