\section{Global Heist Sequence}
\label{sec:ref-global}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Activation:} The Coordinator GM announces activation
        when the clock reaches 4 segments.
  \item \textbf{Declaration (5 minutes):} Each table declares:
        \textbf{Cooperate} / \textbf{Obstruct}.
  \item \textbf{Execution:} All tables roll simultaneously
        (or with a 2-minute offset). Results go to the Coordinator GM.
  \item \textbf{Resolution:} The Coordinator GM counts successes
        and updates the shared clock.
  \item \textbf{Local consequences:} Each table GM narrates
        the consequences in their district.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Notes for the Coordinator GM}
\label{sec:ref-coordinator}

\begin{rulebox}[GoldRPG]{Recommended Session Structure (3 hours)}
\begin{center}
\rowcolors{2}{LightBG}{white}
\begin{tabular}{L{3.5cm} L{2.5cm} p{6.5cm}}
  \toprule
  \textbf{Time} & \textbf{Clock} & \textbf{Narrative phase} \\
  \midrule
  0:00 -- 0:45 & Segments 1--2 & Opening moves; first House Heist attempts \\
  0:45 -- 1:30 & Segments 3--4 & Tension builds; Global Heist activates \\
  1:30 -- 2:15 & Segments 5--6 & Escalation; interference plays \\
  2:15 -- 3:00 & Segments 7--8 & Climax and epilogue \\
  \bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
With 3 tables: reduce to 6 segments.
With 2 tables: reduce to 4 segments and skip the Global Heist.
\end{rulebox}

\begin{rulebox}[GoldRPG]{Real-Time Table Communication}
The Coordinator GM tracks the Shared Clock and manages Global Heist phases.
Recommended tools: \textbf{Discord} (a pinned \texttt{\#shared-clock} channel)
or \textbf{Telegram} (GM-only group). Update the clock visibly after every
significant event so all GMs can see it in real time.
\end{rulebox}

\begin{rulebox}[GoldRPG]{Distributing Character Secrets}
The secret sections on character sheets are marked in red. Distribute sheets
\textbf{individually} before the session, with an explicit instruction not
to share the red section with tablemates.

Secrets are calibrated to create tension \textit{within} the table as well
as between tables. Not all secrets need to surface --- even one or two
revealed in play significantly enrich the narrative.
\end{rulebox}
